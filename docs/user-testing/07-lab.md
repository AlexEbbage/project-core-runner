# Lab User Test

## Goal

Verify the Lab remains the dedicated hub route for combo plus powerup upgrades, with clear costs, immediate profile refresh, and saved progression.

This checklist is the signoff gate for `F18: Long-Term Progression Economy and Lab Upgrades`.

## Setup

- Open the main menu hub.
- Use the `Lab` bottom-nav destination.

## Checklist

- [ ] Confirm the Lab opens from the menu.
- [ ] Confirm the `Ship` destination remains customisation-focused and does not become the primary upgrade surface.
- [ ] Confirm the Lab shows exactly 6 upgrade rows: `Combo Modifier`, `x2 Score`, `x2 Coin Spawn`, `Magnet`, `Autopilot`, `Shield`.
- [ ] Confirm no legacy upgrade rows such as `Handling`, `Pickup Radius`, or `Shield Recharge` appear.
- [ ] Confirm the player soft currency is visible.
- [ ] Upgrade combo once and confirm the level increases and currency decreases.
- [ ] Confirm the top-bar or hub chrome refreshes after the upgrade purchase.
- [ ] Upgrade each powerup once and confirm the level increases and currency decreases.
- [ ] Close and reopen the Lab and confirm all upgrade levels persist.
- [ ] Start a run after upgrading and confirm the upgraded duration/strength is reflected in gameplay.

## Expected Result

The Lab should feel like the dedicated upgrade surface for this slice, with no obsolete upgrade rows leaking through and no confusion with ship loadout browsing.
