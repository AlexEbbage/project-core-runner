# Target Audience Worksheet

Decide this before release.

## Questions

- [ ] Is Core Racer intentionally aimed at children?
- [ ] Is the art/copy likely to appeal to children?
- [ ] Are ads appropriate for the declared audience?
- [ ] Are analytics and ad SDKs configured correctly for the audience?
- [ ] Is parental/family policy compliance required?

If you target children or mixed audiences including children, review ad network settings and Google Play Families policy carefully.
