# Privacy Policy Requirements

Your privacy policy should explain:

- What data is collected
- Why it is collected
- Which third-party SDKs receive data
- Ads and ad personalisation
- Analytics
- Crash reporting
- IAP/payment processor involvement
- Local notifications
- Data retention
- User choices/consent
- Contact/support email
- Data deletion request process

Add the final URL to `PrivacyLinksConfig` and Google Play Console.
